## Getting Started

### Who Can Contribute?

All current students and alumni of IIT Kharagpur are welcome to contribute. We value:

## Contribution Standards

### File Requirements

#### Document Files (PDF, DOCX)

- **Resolution**: Minimum 300 DPI for scanned documents
- **File Size**: Maximum 50 MB per file
- **Naming Convention**: `[CourseCode]_[ResourceType]_[Semester]_[Year].ext`
  - Example: `CS60050_LectureNotes_Autumn_2025.pdf`
- **Legibility**: All text must be clear and readable
- **Completeness**: Include all pages, no missing content

#### Image Files (JPG, PNG)

- **Resolution**: Minimum 1920x1080 for diagrams
- **Format**: PNG for diagrams with text, JPG for photos
- **Compression**: Balance quality and file size
- **Annotations**: Clearly mark any added notes or highlights

#### Video Files (MP4, MOV)

- **Resolution**: Minimum 720p (1080p preferred)
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Audio**: Clear audio with minimal background noise
- **Length**: Split longer videos into segments (< 1 hour each)

### Metadata Requirements

Every submission must include:

1. **Course Information**
   - Course Code (e.g., CS60050)
   - Full Course Name
   - Department

2. **Temporal Information**
   - Semester (Autumn/Spring)
   - Year
   - Week/Module (if applicable)

3. **Instructor Information**
   - Professor's Name
   - Teaching Assistants (if known)

4. **Content Description**
   - Detailed description (50-200 words)
   - Topics covered
   - Related resources (if any)

5. **Resource Type**
   - Lecture Notes
   - Past Year Papers
   - Tutorial Solutions
   - Assignment Problems
   - Reference Materials
   - Video Lectures
   - Lab Materials

## Content Types

### 1. Lecture Notes

**What to Include:**
- Complete notes from lectures
- Professor-provided slides (with permission)
- Handwritten notes (if high quality)
- Supplementary explanations
- Important formulas and theorems
- Example problems and solutions

**Quality Standards:**
- Organized by lecture/topic
- Clear section headings
- Legible handwriting or typed text
- Include date and topic covered
- Reference textbook pages if applicable

### 2. Past Year Papers

**What to Include:**
- Mid-semester examinations
- End-semester examinations
- Quiz papers
- Test papers

**Required Information:**
- Exact semester and year
- Duration and total marks
- Whether solutions are included
- Professor who set the paper

**Important Notes:**
- Only share papers explicitly allowed by faculty
- Mark approximate difficulty level
- Include marking scheme if available

### 3. Tutorial Solutions

**What to Include:**
- Complete worked solutions
- Step-by-step explanations
- Alternative solution methods
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Tips and tricks

**Quality Standards:**
- Show all working clearly
- Explain reasoning at each step
- Highlight key concepts used
- Include diagrams where helpful
- Verify final answers

### 4. Assignments & Projects

**What to Include:**
- Problem statements
- Solution approaches
- Code implementations (if applicable)
- Documentation
- Test cases and results

**Important:**
- Only share after submission deadline
- Respect anti-plagiarism policies
- Include learning resources used
- Document your approach clearly

### 5. Reference Materials

**What to Include:**
- Study guides and summaries
- Cheat sheets
- Formula compilations
- Topic mind maps
- Recommended readings

**Best Practices:**
- Cite all sources properly
- Organize by topic/chapter
- Use clear visual hierarchy
- Include cross-references

## Quality Guidelines

### Document Quality Checklist

- [ ] **Completeness**: All pages present, no missing content
- [ ] **Readability**: Text is clear and legible throughout
- [ ] **Organization**: Logical structure with proper headings
- [ ] **Accuracy**: Content is factually correct
- [ ] **Attribution**: Sources are properly cited
- [ ] **Formatting**: Consistent fonts, spacing, and style
- [ ] **File Quality**: Proper resolution and compression

### Content Quality Standards

#### Excellent (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)
- Comprehensive coverage of all topics
- Clear explanations with examples
- Professional formatting
- Well-organized and indexed
- Includes additional insights
- Minimal to no errors

#### Good (⭐⭐⭐⭐)
- Complete coverage of main topics
- Clear and understandable
- Good formatting
- Organized structure
- Few minor errors

#### Acceptable (⭐⭐⭐)
- Covers essential topics
- Generally understandable
- Basic formatting
- Reasonably organized
- Some errors or gaps

#### Needs Improvement (⭐⭐)
- Incomplete coverage
- Difficult to understand
- Poor formatting
- Disorganized
- Multiple errors

## Copyright & Legal

### What You Can Share

✅ **Allowed:**
- Your own original notes and materials
- Materials explicitly marked for sharing by professors
- Public domain resources
- Materials with Creative Commons licenses
- Past papers shared by faculty
- Your own solutions and work (after deadlines)

❌ **Not Allowed:**
- Copyrighted textbooks without permission
- Materials marked "Do Not Distribute"
- Solutions to current/ongoing assignments
- Proprietary software or tools
- Personal information of students/faculty
- Materials from commercial test prep services

### Citing Sources

Always give credit where it's due:

```
Source: [Professor Name] - [Course Code] Lecture Slides
Based on: [Textbook Name] by [Author], Chapter [X]
Reference: [Research Paper Title] by [Authors]
```

### License Information

By contributing, you agree that:

1. Your content can be shared with IIT Kharagpur students
2. Content is provided for educational purposes only
3. You retain copyright to your original work
4. You grant the AI Society a non-exclusive license to distribute
5. Content may be removed at your or faculty's request

## Submission Process

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Visit the Contribute Page**
   - Navigate to [iit-kgp-ai-society.edu/contribute](/contribute)
   - Log in with your institute credentials (if required)

2. **Fill Out the Form**
   - Enter all required information accurately
   - Double-check course codes and names
   - Write a clear, descriptive title
   - Provide a detailed description

3. **Upload Your File**
   - Use the drag-and-drop interface
   - Verify file size is within limits
   - Confirm file uploaded successfully

4. **Review and Submit**
   - Preview your submission details
   - Verify all information is correct
   - Click "Submit Contribution"

5. **Track Your Submission**
   - Receive confirmation email
   - Check status on your dashboard
   - Respond to any reviewer questions

### Metadata Template

```
Title: [Descriptive Title of Resource]
Course Code: [e.g., CS60050]
Course Name: [e.g., Machine Learning]
Resource Type: [Lecture Notes/Past Papers/etc.]
Semester: [Autumn/Spring] [Year]
Professor: [Prof. Name]
Topics Covered: [Topic 1, Topic 2, Topic 3]
Description: [Detailed description of content, what it covers, 
             how it can help students, any special features]
Additional Notes: [Any other relevant information]
```

## Review Process

### What Happens After Submission

1. **Initial Review (24-48 hours)**
   - Automated checks for file format and size
   - Spam and malware scanning
   - Basic metadata validation

2. **Content Review (2-5 days)**
   - Volunteer reviewers check quality
   - Verify accuracy and completeness
   - Check copyright compliance
   - Ensure metadata is correct

3. **Faculty Approval (if required)**
   - Course instructor notified for sensitive content
   - Past papers require faculty confirmation
   - Special materials need explicit permission

4. **Publication**
   - Approved content goes live
   - You receive confirmation notification
   - Content appears in search results
   - Contributors are credited

### Possible Outcomes

✅ **Approved**: Content published immediately

⏸️ **Revision Needed**: Reviewers provide feedback, resubmit after changes

⚠️ **Pending Faculty Approval**: Waiting for instructor confirmation

❌ **Rejected**: Does not meet quality standards or copyright issues

## Best Practices

### Do's ✅

- Upload content immediately after semester ends
- Keep file names descriptive and organized
- Include comprehensive descriptions
- Credit all sources properly
- Update outdated materials
- Respond to student feedback
- Help review other contributions
- Report copyright issues

### Don'ts ❌

- Don't upload current semester assignments before deadline
- Don't share materials without permission
- Don't upload copyrighted textbooks
- Don't include personal information
- Don't submit low-quality scans

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Can I contribute anonymously?**
A: No, all contributions must be attributed for quality control and recognition.

**Q: How long does review take?**
A: Most submissions are reviewed within 3-5 business days.

**Q: Can I edit my submission after it's published?**
A: Yes, contact the review team with your updated version.

**Q: What if my professor doesn't want content shared?**
A: We respect faculty wishes. Content will be removed immediately upon request.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What file formats are accepted?**
A: PDF, DOCX, DOC, JPG, PNG, MP4, and MOV.

**Q: Is there a file size limit?**
A: Yes, 50 MB per file. For larger files, split into parts.

**Q: Can I upload multiple files at once?**
A: Not currently, but this feature is coming soon.

### Content Questions

**Q: Should I include solutions to practice problems?**
A: Yes, if they're from past semesters and you have permission.

**Q: Can I share handwritten notes?**
A: Yes, if they're legible and high-quality scans (300 DPI minimum).

**Q: What if I'm not sure about copyright?**
A: When in doubt, don't upload. Contact us for clarification.
